Experiment No. 6 Data Aggregation and Statistical Functions in Tableau

Aim: To perform data aggregation and apply statistical functions in Tableau for better data analysis.

Description: Data aggregation combines multiple records into summary values. Common statistical measures include sum, average, minimum and maximum. Tableau can perform these operations directly on data fields.

Uses: • Finding total sales. • Calculating average values. • Finding minimum and maximum values. • Comparing categories. • Summarizing large datasets.

Algorithm:

Open Tableau Public.
Connect to the dataset.
Drag required fields into rows and columns.
Apply aggregation functions.
Use statistical measures.
Analyze and save the results.
